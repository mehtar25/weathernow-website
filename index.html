About the app

WeatherNow is an Android weather app developed by CMPT 395 Team 5. Search for a city, view current conditions, explore hourly and seven-day forecasts, and choose Celsius or Fahrenheit.

WeatherNow is completely free, with no advertisements or in-app purchases.
