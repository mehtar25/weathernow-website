WeatherNow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

WeatherNow is maintained by CMPT 395 Team 5. It provides weather forecasts for locations you select. The app is free, with no advertisements or in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

WeatherNow saves your selected city, temperature-unit preference, and cached weather forecasts on your device. This allows the app to remember your settings and display previously downloaded forecasts when a refresh fails.

You can remove this local information by clearing the app's storage through Android settings. Depending on your device settings and the app's backup configuration, Android may back up and restore app data.

Weather and city searches

WeatherNow sends your city-search text to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. When you select a location, the app sends that location's coordinates and timezone to Open-Meteo to request forecasts.

These coordinates describe the location you selected. WeatherNow does not access your phone's GPS location. Network requests also expose your IP address to the service provider.

Open-Meteo states that it may retain technical logs, including IP addresses and geographical coordinates, for troubleshooting and abuse prevention, and deletes those logs after 90 days.

Read Open-Meteo's terms and privacy information .

Permissions and tracking

WeatherNow uses internet access to retrieve forecasts and search for cities. It does not require an account or access your microphone, contacts, or device location. This weather-only version does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Support emails

Contact support opens your email application. A message is sent only when you choose to send it. If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use that information to respond to your request.

Support emails are handled through our Gmail inbox and retained while needed to address your request and related follow-up. You may contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change and revise the date above.
